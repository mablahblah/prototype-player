# Oklahoma Teen Drivers Ed Course

A stand-in course outline for the RA prototypes: 6 levels, each with a different number of chapters. Titles only.

Each prototype folder in the RA Prototype Set keeps its own copy of this file.

1. Welcome to Drivers Ed
2. Getting Your Learner's Permit
3. Know Your Vehicle
4. Seat, Mirrors and Controls
5. Level 1 Review

## Level 2 — Rules of the Road

3. Right-of-Way
4. Speed Limits
5. Intersections
6. Roundabouts
7. Turning and Lane Use
8. Parking Rules
9. Level 2 Review

## Level 3 — Driving Skills

2. Steering Basics
3. Backing Up
4. Changing Lanes and Merging
5. Parallel Parking
6. Following Distance
7. Level 3 Review

## Level 4 — Sharing the Road

1. Pedestrians and Crosswalks
2. Cyclists and Motorcycles
3. Large Trucks and Buses
4. School Zones and School Buses
5. Emergency Vehicles
6. Level 4 Review

## Level 5 — Hazards and Conditions

2. Rain and Fog
